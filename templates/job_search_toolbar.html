The limit applies before description keyword matching. Larger searches take longer, and job boards may return fewer results. Choose Last 24 hours in your search profile's Dates posted field for recent jobs. A larger limit does not guarantee every matching job is fetched.

Indeed and Glassdoor need a supported country. ZipRecruiter covers US/Canada, BDJobs Bangladesh, and Naukri India. Description keywords must all match.
